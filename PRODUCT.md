# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS without a build step (`index.html`, `price.html`, `assets/`), published by GitHub Pages from the root of `main` — the owner's standard format for redesigns (same as jdmmotors, stozapad, tolex).

## Users

Owners of Volkswagen, Audi, Škoda, SEAT, Porsche and Bentley cars in Minsk (cars 2006 and newer) looking for a specialist independent service as an alternative to the official dealer. They usually arrive with a concrete problem (DSG jerks, warning light, scheduled service, pre-purchase check) on a phone, compare price/trust, then call or message to book. Secondary: companies with fleets (cash and bank transfer, VAT).

## Product Purpose

VAGMOTORS (ООО «ВАГМОТОРС») is a specialist service centre for VAG-group cars in central Minsk: from a bulb replacement to engine overhaul, DSG and mechatronic repair, diagnostics with dealer software (VAS 6150D), sale and fitting of original and licensed parts. Success = the visitor books a visit (call, Telegram, Viber, WhatsApp).

## Positioning

Single-group specialisation (VAG only) with dealer-grade tools and factory procedures, at a more humane price than the dealer, in the city centre, open every day 9:00–21:00 — and the client is kept informed and can watch the work.

## Operating Context

- Booking happens by phone or messenger; prices depend on model/engine/gearbox, so the site lists "from" prices and asks for make, model, year, engine and ideally VIN.
- Parts are sold only on condition they are fitted at the station.
- Works only with cars not older than 2006.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static hosting (GitHub Pages): no server; the booking form and the calculator's pre-check compose a message and open Telegram/Viber/WhatsApp or a call.
- Services: diagnostics, electrics, maintenance, engine, DSG/S tronic/PDK, suspension, brakes, A/C, parts.
- Warranty: maintenance 1 month / 2 000 km; repair with licensed parts 6 months / 10 000 km; with original parts 12 months / 20 000 km; client-supplied parts — labour only, 1 month / 2 000 km (whichever comes first).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: VAGMOTORS / VAG Motors / Вагмоторс. Logo: red "VAG" + dark "MOTORS" wordmark (`/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/Inked10-1.png`, `/2023/10/logo-2-2.png` on vagmotors.by).
- Owner asked for "VAG colours" and an expensive, modern look matching the group's premium image.
- Manufacturer marks (VW, Audi, Škoda, SEAT, Porsche, Bentley) are used only to name the makes serviced; the site must not imply it is an official dealer.

## Product Principles

1. Specialist, not generalist: every surface proves VAG depth (DSG series, dealer software, original parts).
2. Transparency over hype: "from" prices, explicit warranty terms, real photos and real reviews.
3. One step to booking from anywhere on the page, in the messenger the client already uses.
4. Never present as the official dealer.
